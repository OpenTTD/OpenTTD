## Reading this result

**This run does not measure what it was meant to, and B won: 368425 to A's 265599.**

Bridge 31fdb68 lets the bot plan its own next freight lines once every line handed to it was tried. Its
setting `own_lines` defaulted to 8. Company B is the same PrismaticAI started without a plan. It was
handed no lines, so "every handed line tried" was true from the first month, and B planned and built
its own rail from the start: seven trains by month 24. The baseline was no longer an unplanned bot. Both
companies then competed for the same industries. A had one missing path and one lost train, and ended
far below its run without the change (duel-20261117T112500: 995647).

What it shows by accident: **the bot's own planner alone took B from about 27000 (every earlier
unplanned B on this map) to 368425.** It picks producing industries nobody serves, scored by the
game's own payment rates. A's own planner ran once (a grain line, score 105) after its handed lines.

Fix: `own_lines` defaults to 0, so a bot started without a plan stays the unplanned baseline. The panel
sets `own_lines` only for the bot it starts from a plan. The own plan is measured again under those
conditions.
