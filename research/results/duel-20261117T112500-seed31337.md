## Reading this result

A won **995647 to 27870 (35.7 to 1)** with fourteen trains: the best result on this map so far, and
**+67% over the same bot and plan** in the previous run (duel-20261117T100202: 597075).

The only change is the script budget. The panel had always meant to give scripts 250000 opcodes a tick
(script.script_max_opcode_till_suspend), the game's maximum. It set the value with the console's
`setting` after the world was made, and the game refused it, because the setting is NewgameOnly. So
**every earlier duel ran the bots at the default 10000**. The panel now writes the budget into
openttd.cfg before the world is made (1d8c45fa77) and logs the value it got ("script budget: ... is
'250000'"). Both companies get the same budget. The rival's value is unchanged (29139 then, 27870 now),
because it builds a few bus stops and no rail.

The planned bot plans its rail paths in Squirrel, so the budget was its bottleneck. It built:

- **with 10000:** one line every two to three months (eleven trains by month 36, money waiting);
- **with 250000:** all fourteen lines handed over by month 24 (13 freight and the passenger pair), with
  no failed piece and no lost train.
